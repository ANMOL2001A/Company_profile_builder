About Cynoteck Technology Solutions Private Limited
Cynoteck Technology Solutions Private Limited  is a Private Limited Company and was incorporated on Oct 07, 2013 in India.
It is registered at Registrar of Companies, ROC Uttarakhand.
Its registered address is PLOT NO.4, MOUNT VIEW COLONY NEAR IT PARK, SAHASTRADHARA ROAD DEHRADUN , Dehradun Unclassified UR 248001.
Its  corporate identification number is (CIN) U72300UR2013PTC000887 and its current status is Active.
Its industry classification as per MCA is data processing. [This includes the processing or tabulation of all types of data. Provision of such services on (i) an hourly or time -share basis, and (ii) management or operation of data processing facilities of others on a time sharing basis; on a fee or contract basis].
It had a team size of 42 employees as on Jan 31, 2025.
Cynoteck Technology Solutions Private Limited's authorised share capital is Rs. 100,000 and its paid up capital is Rs. 100,000.
Cynoteck Technology Solutions Private Limited generated a revenue of Rs. 7.48Cr for the financial year ending on Mar 31, 2022.
