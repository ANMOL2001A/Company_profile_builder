About Us
We believe in the power of technology and provide exceptional experiences. Our right teams can take care of your technological aspects and amplify your business productivity.
Contact Us
We are increasingly impressed by the great customer service and kindness - Joseph Adams, Attorney & Shareholder
Cynoteck is a dynamic and professional IT services provider that serves enterprises and individuals, helping them meet the challenges of the global economy. We offer services in the area of CRM Consultation and implementation, Application development, Mobile application development, Product Engineering, Digital Transformation, AI, Data Analytics, and Web development & Offshore Development. Our experienced Project Managers apply a mature development methodology, continuously exceeding our client’s business expectations, and ensuring the success of offshoring projects. We have expertise ranging from consulting on business and technology to creating innovative technology solutions. Our vision shapes the culture at Cynoteck, which lays a strong emphasis on innovation and thought process.
Cynoteck’s name is derived from Cynosure + Technology + Knowledge. We deliver value to our customers by utilizing our technology and knowledge. We believe that IT should be a highly value-adding asset to businesses that rely on technology to provide a competitive advantage in their industry. To do so, IT must be treated as an integral part of the business. To be successful as a business, IT must have a clear vision and a realistic plan that is aligned with corporate goals. It also must have an IT organization that is able to leverage the infrastructure to execute those plans and manage them effectively.
WORK CULTURE
“Work while you work and play while you play” defines Cynoteck work culture. We work hard 5 days a week and come together once every month on 1st Saturday for learning and fun. These Saturdays are filled with Knowledge sharing sessions, quizzes, sports, and other team building activities. We follow the 80/20 policy of 80 percent of an employee’s time is dedicated to work and 20 percent towards self-development and team building activities.
OUR HISTORY
Cynoteck undertook its journey in 2008 with a humble team of three individuals and two determined founders, Udit Handa, and Anshul Verma. Their collective vision was to establish a world-class IT service company, choosing Dehradun as one of the locations for their delivery centre due to the city's abundant talent pool and potential for growth despite limited opportunities. Headquartered in
Minnesota, USA
, the company strategically set up its offshore centre in
Dehradun, India, and Dubai, UAE
to leverage the skilled workforce and promote global collaboration.
Over the years, Cynoteck's team of dedicated consultants has made significant strides, undertaking numerous globally recognized software implementations and pioneering innovations. This continuous progress has enriched their expertise, enabling them to craft exceptional outsourced solutions and top-notch product development services.
